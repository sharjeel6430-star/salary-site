UK Salary Tools: income tax threshold freeze data pack (2026/27 and 2027/28)
https://www.uksalarytools.co.uk/research/income-tax-threshold-freeze-2026

Free to use with a credit and link to uksalarytools.co.uk (CC BY 4.0).

Files
- thresholds_frozen_vs_indexed.csv: personal allowance and 40% threshold, frozen vs if uprated by the previous September's CPI, 2021/22 to 2027/28.
- freeze_cost_by_salary.csv: income tax and employee NI with frozen and with indexed thresholds, and the extra paid because of the freeze, by salary, 2026/27 and 2027/28. Negative NI means the frozen upper earnings limit saves NI.
- same_real_pay_worker.csv: a 2021/22 salary raised in line with CPI to 2026/27, its tax band now and with indexed thresholds, the extra income tax in 2026/27 and in total over 2022/23 to 2026/27.
- minimum_wage_and_allowance.csv: National Living Wage (23+ until March 2024, 21+ from April 2024), hours and full-time weeks (37.5 hours) covered by the £12,570 allowance, full-time pay, income tax frozen and if indexed, and tax as a share of pay.
- chart1_extra_tax_by_salary.png, chart2_thresholds.png, chart3_minimum_wage_weeks.png

Method
Thresholds uprated each April by the previous September's CPI (ONS), as income tax law sets by default: personal allowance rounded up to the next £10, basic rate limit to the next £100. England, Wales and Northern Ireland rates. Today's 20/40/45% income tax and 8/2% employee NI rates in every year. The £100,000 allowance taper point and the £125,140 additional-rate threshold are kept as they are. NI primary threshold and upper earnings limit move with the allowance and the 40% threshold, as they have been aligned since 2022/23. No pension, student loan or other deductions.

Sources: ONS consumer price inflation; GOV.UK income tax rates and thresholds; GOV.UK National Minimum Wage and National Living Wage rates; GOV.UK policy paper on maintaining thresholds until 5 April 2031.
